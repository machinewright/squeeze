## Description

Each level asks for the most, or the fewest: how many pairs can dance at once, how many dominoes fit on the board,
how much water flows through the pipes, how few stacks hold all the boxes.

Finding a good answer is half the job. The other half is proving that nobody can do better:
people who touch every possible pairing, pipes whose closing stops all the water, boxes that can never share a stack.
A meter shows the range the true answer could be in. Your answer pushes it up from below, your proof pushes it down
from above, and the level is solved when they meet.

You never know the target in advance, so you can't just stop at a number: you have to squeeze it.

- 19 levels in 5 chapters: Pairs, Dominoes, Pipes, Boxes, and Without rows
- The proofs are four classic theorems (König, Ford and Fulkerson, Dilworth, Tutte and Berge), played rather than stated
- Every answer is computed exactly, and the game checks your answer and your proof itself
- Mouse, touch and keyboard
