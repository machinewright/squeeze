Squeeze

Each level asks for the most, or the fewest. Finding a good answer is half the job. The other half is proving nobody can do better. A level is solved when your answer and your proof meet.

Every level's answer is checked by an exact algorithm, and the game checks your answer and your proof itself, so it can't be fooled. Your progress stays in this browser.
